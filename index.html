About these numbers

Where the data comes from

  • Flatfox public listings API: price, area, rooms, floor, year built, location.
  • BFS Leerwohnungszählung 2026: empty-dwelling rate per Gemeinde, counted on 1 June.
  • swisstopo commune boundaries: which Gemeinde each listing is in.
  • transport.opendata.ch (SBB timetable): fastest of four trips to Zürich HB.

How it is worked out

  • Door to door = walking to the nearest stop at 12 min per km (straight line) + the trip from that stop.
  • Each criterion becomes a percentile among the listings that pass your limits, then the weights average them into a 0–100 score.
  • Year built comes from the listing, or from the ad text ("Baujahr 1998"). "Erstbezug" or "Neubau" without a year counts as 2026.

What it misses

  • Flatfox only. Homegate and ImmoScout24 carry most sale listings but offer no public API.
  • Asking prices, not sale prices. Ads without a price are left out.
  • Vacancy counts all dwellings, rented and owned. Tax rates, Nebenkosten and renovation funds are not in yet.
